The Happy Floor 💛

Little things that keep our dance floor fun, kind and safe for everyone — inspired by our neighbours in Hà Nội & Sài Gòn.

At a Swing Cool Kidz social, everyone should feel welcome and safe. These simple guidelines help us share the floor with care — please read them and help us keep the good vibes going!
📜 The Happy Floor — 12 guidelines

🤝 Just ask

Anyone can ask anyone to dance — no partner needed. A smile and a simple “Would you like to dance?” is all it takes. A “no thanks” is completely fine; no explanation needed, and no hard feelings.

🪄 Ask before the extras

Want to dip, do a close move, or something a little adventurous? Ask first — every time. Consent is an ongoing conversation, not a one-off, so check in during the dance too.

🧭 Give space

Whoever is travelling backwards has right of way. Keep your moves small when the floor is busy, and keep an eye on the dancers around you.

🚫 No surprise aerials

Flips and air steps need consent, space and practice — never over a crowded floor. If in doubt, save them for the edge of the room when there's space, and agree first.

🧼 Come fresh

Fresh shirt, deodorant, and a spare if you sweat a lot. Comfortable shoes, clean soft hands, tidy nails — and easy on the strong perfume (allergies!).

🦋 Dance gently

Lead and follow kindly — no yanking, forcing or squeezing. If something hurts or feels off, speak up. We're here to enjoy ourselves, not to win anything.

📚 Not your teacher

We all love a good tip, but unsolicited coaching can spoil a dance. Save teaching for class, and if a partner asks for feedback, keep it kind and specific.

🎵 Dance the song

Keep the dance to one song unless you both fancy more. Always thank your partner — a hug, a high-five or a “that was fun!” goes a long way.

🌱 Welcome the new kids

Dance with beginners and newcomers — we were all new once. Introduce yourself, and help make someone's very first social a great one.

📸 Photos with consent

Ask before filming or photographing people, and be mindful of anyone who'd rather stay off camera.

🏠 Love the space

Buy a drink or food, tidy up after yourself, and tell us about spills (slippery!). Be kind to the venue staff — they make our home possible.

🚨 A safe space

Harassment of any kind isn't welcome here. If anything feels uncomfortable — for you or for someone else — tell an organiser. We look after each other.

Good Questions

Quick answers about swing dancing in Đà Nẵng & Hội An.

Where can I dance swing / Lindy Hop in Đà Nẵng?

Swing Cool Kidz runs swing socials in Đà Nẵng: Lindy Hop at Blends Social Bar (every other Thursday, with a beginner taster class) and live-jazz socials at the Hyatt Regency Đà Nẵng — Terrasse Bar (every other Wednesday). We also connect with the scenes in Hà Nội and Sài Gòn.

Do I need a partner or any experience?

Nope! Everyone is welcome — no partner and no experience needed. A 45-minute taster class runs before the Blends social to get you started.

How much does it cost?

At Blends, entry is 70k including one drink (Huda or water). At Hyatt Terrasse there's no entrance fee — just buy a drink or food from the menu.

When & where are the socials?

Blends: every other Thursday, 7–9PM. Hyatt Terrasse: every other Wednesday, 8–10PM. They alternate week to week — see the calendar for exact dates.

Can I bring a friend who doesn't dance?

Absolutely — friends are welcome to hang out. At Blends they pay the same entry (70k incl. a drink); at Hyatt the bar is open to outside guests, just buy from the menu.
